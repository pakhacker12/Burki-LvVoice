BURKI LIVE Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

BURKI LIVE is a social voice chat application where users can connect with friends, join voice rooms and chat.

Information We Collect

We may collect usernames, account information, profile information and information required to provide app features.

Microphone

Microphone access is used only when users choose to participate in voice rooms.

Social Login

Users may choose to sign in using supported third-party services. We receive only the information permitted by the user's authorization.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide accounts, friends, voice rooms, chat and app security.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the BURKI LIVE app team.
